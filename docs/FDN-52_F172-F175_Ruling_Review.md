## Consistency findings

1. **F172 is independently implementable.** Its same-device record and existing-device transfer do not depend on SLIP-0039. Enrollment authorization and relay delivery are explicit consumed boundaries rather than work invented inside FDN-52.
2. **F173 no longer requires an ad-hoc FDN-52 database transaction design.** The only storage expansion is an FDN-84-owned atomic CAS over one sealed record. FDN-52 performs copy-on-write above that interface. Complete historical-store rollback remains explicitly unclaimed without a trusted monotonic anchor.
3. **F174 required one additional direct specification correction.** VPS-F007 still assigned cryptographic erasure to Tier 2. Its directly affected sentences and criteria now match A003: Tier 0/2 redaction/deletion/cache purge is weaker; Tier 1/3 key destruction is cryptographic erasure. No Tier 2 encryption architecture was added.
4. **F175 narrows the claim without weakening Tier 3.** Worker-private protected state remains mandatory. The only exception is transient browser-required ceremony material in a trusted Window path, with arbitrary same-origin execution during the ceremony outside the isolation claim. The production generic sealed-record interface is removed rather than treated as authorization.
5. **No new contradiction requires a Founder ruling before code.** The remaining cross-issue boundaries are interfaces with settled ownership: FDN-84 CAS/persistence, FDN-63/89 enrollment authorization, FDN-51 delivery and future rollback anchor, and VPS-F007 workflow/cache/audit work.

## Assurance limit retained

Atomic CAS can prove crash consistency and serialize concurrent live Workers. It cannot detect replacement of the entire local database with an older internally valid snapshot after all volatile state is gone. FDN-52 must not describe the result as full rollback resistance. A future monotonic anchor may improve connected-device detection, but offline commits cannot be perfectly remotely anchored until connectivity returns.

## Pre-code decision

The amendments form a consistent `specification → property → owner → implementation → evidence` chain. Implementation may proceed for the FDN-52-owned portions and the smallest FDN-84 CAS interface. F167 and all SLIP-0039 work remain excluded.
