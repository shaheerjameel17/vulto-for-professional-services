# Foundations Findings

**Status:** Open. The Core Engineering and Graph Foundations phase is in progress.
**Purpose:** Record every defect, ambiguity and contradiction found in the specification set by implementing it, so corrections go back into the document that owns the fact.

This file is not a specification and is deliberately outside `docs/Vulto_Specs/`. It is the successor to `docs/Prototype_Findings.md`, which closed at fifty-three findings.

**Finding numbers continue from that log rather than restarting.** A finding ID is unique across the whole project, so `F27` means one thing and only one thing regardless of which log a reader is holding.

**The two logs were produced by different instruments.** The prototype rendered four design documents and found what could only be found by looking at a screen. This phase implements two architecture documents and finds what can only be found by trying to turn prose into types — a column that cannot be parsed, a mapping with holes in it, a relationship with no edge to traverse. Neither instrument would have found the other's findings.

## F130 — the live gap is closed (Stage 8, RST-33), with one stated limit

**F130 was a live, present-tense gap: Owner and HR Admin saw every HR case, including one filed about themselves, because no User-to-Employee link existed to enforce `VPS-A004`'s subject exclusion (A004-T16).** RST-33 builds the link (an Employee's `user_id`, set only by `employee.linkUser`) and the read-path enforcement. `decideRead` now removes the subject from a record's readers for every node type that registers an exclusion, and that one decision feeds the interceptor, `protected.read` and the materialized sync audience, as A004-T16 requires. It is proven by paired tests (`services/api/src/employee/employee.integration.test.ts`): the subject is restricted with the reason *"Restricted — this record concerns you."*, every other permitted reader still reads, and mutation-testing the exclusion out fails two of them.

**The limit, stated so it is not discovered.** The exclusion applies to the login *linked* to the subject's Employee record. A person who is the subject of a case but whose login is not yet linked to their Employee record (employment and system access are separate facts; the link is set when an invitation is accepted) cannot be recognized as the subject and is not excluded. That is inherent in identifying a person by a link, and it is recorded in F215 rather than left implicit.
