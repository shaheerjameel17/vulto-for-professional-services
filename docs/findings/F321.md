### F321 — the notification registry proves Tier 0, not "no money"

**Found while:** the reviewer's independent review of the merged Stage 25 diff, 26 September 2026.

The Stage 25 brief promised, and its review checklist told the reviewer to confirm, that the notification registry would refuse a message that references money, by adding `{source.daily_cost}` to a template. It does not do that and cannot: the registry's proof (`fieldsAreTier0`, factored out of `validateSearchableNodeTypes`) is per node partition, and `RevenueGapAlert` is a fixed Tier 0 node, so `daily_cost` and `accumulated_cost` are Tier 0 fields a rule may legitimately declare. The builder's negative control (the template edited to `{source.daily_cost}` without declaring it) fails on "Undeclared notification template field", which proves the declaration rule, not a money rule. What keeps money out of the two shipped messages is the shipped templates and the message-disclosure test, and what keeps it out of future rules is review of each new registration (the registry is open by design).

**Files:** `packages/schema/src/notification-rules.ts`, `packages/schema/src/search.ts`, `services/api/src/permission/notification-message.test.ts`

**Status.** **Recorded fact, 26 September 2026, no fix.** The guarantee is "message fields are Tier 0 and declared", not "no money". A rule that declares a Tier 0 money field is a reviewer decision at registration time. Recorded so it is not mistaken for a stronger guarantee, and so the reviewer's own checklist item (which asked for the wrong control) is corrected: the useful control is that a `source` field from a Tier 1 or Tier 2 source, and any protected subject field, is refused, which the registry test does prove.
