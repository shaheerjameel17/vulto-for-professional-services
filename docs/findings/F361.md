### F361 — the 38 prototype/UI-1 review screenshots were visual-QA evidence only and had no lasting need

**Found while:** An independent audit (28 September 2026, migrated into Notion's Vulto Documentation database) flagged that 38 screenshots referenced from specs and findings could not be uploaded to Notion (a local-image/signed-upload-token restriction) and remained reachable only via their original GitHub links. Raised with the founder the same day; he ruled it does not need solving.

Two directories held these: `artifacts/prototype-findings/` (24 files, prototype-phase design evidence for FDN-4–FDN-33-era findings) and `docs/stage-reports/ui-1/` (14 files, the seven-state/two-theme UI-1 acceptance screenshots F345 specified and the build prompt's Do item 7 required). Both were git-tracked, committed, and referenced by filename — not embedded — from `docs/Prototype_Findings.md`, `docs/Claude_Code_Build_Prompt.md` and `docs/findings/F345.md`, as evidence that a specific visual state was captured and reviewed at the time.

**Files:** `artifacts/prototype-findings/` (24 files), `docs/stage-reports/ui-1/` (14 files)

**Ruling.** **Closed 28 September 2026 by founder ruling.** The images were a one-time visual check for front-end work, not a permanent product record, and Notion has no genuine need for them either — there is no requirement to solve the upload gap. All 38 files were deleted from the repository (`git rm`, both directories, nothing left behind). The prose in `docs/Prototype_Findings.md`, `docs/Claude_Code_Build_Prompt.md` and `docs/findings/F345.md` that names specific filenames as evidence is left exactly as written: it is historical record describing what was captured and verified at the time, and per this project's own convention findings are not retroactively edited once ruled. A reader following one of those filenames after this ruling will find it no longer exists in the repository; that is expected, not a defect.
