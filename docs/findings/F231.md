### F231 — G07's non-deferred exceptions have no Stage 12 consumer or write path

F226 defers only the materialized index and its rebuild mechanism. Its detailed ruling explicitly says G07's three exceptions are *not* deferred and that Stage 12 proves them directly. `VRS-F004`'s acceptance criteria require a closed PayRun to remain unchanged with an adjustment raised in the next period; an approved LeaveRequest to keep its deduction while a notice reaches the approving manager; and a signed Contract to remain unchanged. The repository has registered node types and permission tests for these concepts but no PayRun, LeaveRequest or Contract feature writer, no next-period adjustment operation, and no manager-notice workflow for this event. Stage 12 Do item 5 only defines `workingDays.*` read functions, which will return the new confirmed hours on their next call. That proves the live-read half of G07, but neither creates an adjustment nor sends a notice, and a test that confirms no unrelated row changed would not prove the required effects. The brief gives no hook or deferred owner for them.

**Ruling — closed 22 September 2026, correcting F226's own overreach.** F226 said G07's three recalculation exemptions "are not deferred... Stage 12 proves them directly." That was wrong on inspection: F226 was right that a closed PayRun, an approved LeaveRequest and a signed Contract must each be *exempt* from recalculation, but proving an exemption requires the thing being exempted to exist, and none of `VRS-F062` (Payroll), leave's `LeaveRequest`, or `VRS-F020` (Contract) is built. Stage 12 has no PayRun, LeaveRequest or Contract writer, no next-period adjustment operation and no manager-notice workflow to test against — F231 is correct that a plain `workingDays.*` re-read cannot by itself raise either the adjustment or the notice, because nothing consumes `workingDays.*` at that granularity yet. Corrected split: Stage 12 proves only the general case G07's own text already separates from the three exemptions — "everything else... recalculates freely, because each is a live view rather than a committed record" — by confirming `workingDays.count`/`isWorking`/`hoursOn` return the newly-confirmed values on the very next call after a `holiday.confirm`, with nothing cached or stale. The three named exemptions (PayRun, LeaveRequest, Contract) are documented in `VRS-F004` as binding rules for whichever stage builds each consuming feature — each of those stages inherits the obligation to implement its own exemption and its own adjustment/notice behavior against a calendar shift, exactly as it inherits every other rule this document states about its own node type — but none of the three is Stage 12's to build or test. `VRS-F004`'s G07 and its acceptance criteria are corrected to mark the PayRun/LeaveRequest/Contract examples as forward-looking rules for their own future stages rather than Stage 12 acceptance criteria, and F226's own ruling paragraph and Stage 12's Done criteria are both corrected to match this narrower, accurate scope.

**Status: closed.** Stage 12 is unblocked; the resolution-order/write-model work (F227–F230) and the live-view half of G07 are all it needs to prove.
