### F315 — the compliance view has no role gate for the reminder to reuse

**Found while:** Codex tracing Stage 25 Do item 5 before writing product code, 26 September 2026 (stage report `8b63851`); ruled by the reviewer the same day.

The Stage 25 brief (Do item 5) said `hrCompliance.sendReminder` must reuse "the role gate `hrCompliance.listSubmissionStatus` already enforces". That gate does not exist: `services/api/src/router.ts:353` registers the query as an ordinary `protectedProcedure`, and `services/api/src/permission/timesheet-queries.ts::listSubmissionStatus` filters Active Employees through `filterReadable` and `withinRoleScopedReach` for `TimesheetEntry`, rejecting no role, and `timesheet.integration.test.ts` deliberately allows a Team Member to see their own row. The reviewer had asserted a gate without reading the query; the compliance view is read-scoped, not role-gated.

**Files:** `services/api/src/router.ts`, `services/api/src/permission/timesheet-queries.ts`, `packages/schema/src/policy/policy-table.ts`, `docs/Vulto_Specs/VRS-F010_Timesheet_Speed-Run.md`

**Ruling.** **Closed 26 September 2026 by ruling.** The spec says the reminder belongs to an HR Admin's view and "nobody but the employee and an HR Admin writes". The sender must therefore hold, in at least one role, a `full` cell with scope `any` for `TimesheetEntry` in the policy table, resolved through `resolvePolicyCell` and not a hard-coded role list (Owner and HR Admin today; Manager and Finance Admin are `read`, Team Member is `full` but `own`, so none may send, including to themselves). The mutation may then name only employees `listSubmissionStatus` returns for that caller with a `Draft` or `Not-Started` week; any other id is rejected as a nonexistent one is. No change to the query's read contract. Lesson: a brief must not assert that a guard exists without reading the code that would enforce it.
