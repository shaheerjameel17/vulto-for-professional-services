### F330 — the policy year is undefined

**Found while:** briefing Stage 27 (`VRS-F018`), 26 September 2026, by checking the spec against the code before writing the brief.

The spec speaks of "the policy year" (monthly accrual, annual grant, carry-over) and never defines it; there is no fiscal-year field on `LeavePolicy`, `Entity` or `Workspace`.

**Files:** `docs/Vulto_Specs/VRS-F018_Leave_Policy_Engine.md`, `packages/schema/src/mutations/entity.ts`

**Ruling.** **Closed 26 September 2026 by ruling.** The policy year is the UTC calendar year (1 January to 31 December) at MVP. A per-entity fiscal-year start is a later, additive field; the engine takes the policy-year start as a function of the date so that change stays local.
