### F337 — `standard_daily_hours` and the week's expected hours are undefined for irregular patterns

**Found while:** briefing Stage 28, 27 September 2026.

The formula `(logged − expected) / standard_daily_hours × rate` says `standard_daily_hours` comes from the employee's working calendar, but a part-time or four-day pattern has no single "standard day", and `expected_hours` is not defined beyond the `HoursExceedExpected` check's own weekly sum.

**Files:** `services/api/src/mutations/timesheet-anomaly.ts`, `services/api/src/graph/working-days.ts`

**Ruling.** **Closed 27 September 2026 by ruling.** `expected_hours` is the sum of `resolvedDayOn(...).hours` over the flag's seven days (the sum `HoursExceedExpected` already uses; one shared helper, moved not copied). `standard_daily_hours` is `expected_hours ÷ the number of working days among those seven`: a part-timer's day is their day (`G05`). A week with no working days accrues nothing, with an explicit reason. `logged_hours` is the sum of the week's `Submitted`, non-`Pitch` entries, matching the rule that flagged it. No calendar-day arithmetic.
