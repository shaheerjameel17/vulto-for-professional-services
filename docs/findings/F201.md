### F201 — the replacement sync engine is Electric, not PowerSync

Raised and closed on 20 September 2026, hours after F199, when the founder asked the direct question: *is PowerSync paid, and if so replace it.*

**What checking found.** PowerSync's client SDKs are Apache-2.0 and its Open Edition service is free to run, but the service is licensed under the **Functional Source License**, which permits any use except a "Competing Use" — defined partly as a product offering "substantially similar functionality". Vulto is an HR product, not a sync product, so the permission holds on a fair reading; the objection is that it is a reading, and a pre-revenue company should not carry a license question it may one day have to argue. More concretely, the Open Edition requires **MongoDB** for its sync buckets in addition to PostgreSQL: a second datastore to run, back up, restore and keep alive in every residency region `VPS-A008` P8 promises.

**Decision.** Electric (Apache-2.0) for the read path. It replicates PostgreSQL to clients over HTTP as filtered shapes, needs nothing but the database it replicates from, and is self-hostable anywhere. Electric has no write path and no local store, which is acceptable because writes already go through Vulto's named mutations and the local cache was always ours to shape: `packages/graph` keeps the SQLite-WASM cache, and adds the outbox with idempotent retry, optimistic application and rollback, and SyncStatus. Roughly two weeks of TypeScript in exchange for one fewer datastore, no license ambiguity, and a stack a customer's own infrastructure team could run.

**What this changes in the documents.** `VPS-A001`'s sync client selection and A001-T02/T04; `VPS-A003`'s topology and its replication decision; `VPS-A007`'s development image, compose stack, deploy order and A007-T19; `CLAUDE.md`'s stack and phase list. `wa-sqlite` stays in the stack; `loro-crdt` is still retired.

**What does not change.** Nothing about tiers, permissions, encryption, offline behavior or the trust program. The shape conformance gate, the publication allowlist and "no Tier 1 or Tier 2 data on a device" all stand; only the component streaming the rows is different.
