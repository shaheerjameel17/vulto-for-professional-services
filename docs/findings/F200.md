### F200 — trust becomes a program of verifiable promises

**Founder decision, 20 September 2026, with F199.** `VPS-A008` is new. It replaces "Vulto cannot read your data" with eight promises a customer can check: no staff access without Owner approval; a customer-visible Access Transparency log; field-level encryption of pay and HR data; customer-managed keys on Enterprise; a hash-chained, daily-anchored audit log with a published verifier; the permission policy published and its running hash shown in the product; an open export format; and a residency region per workspace on Enterprise. It fixes external-assurance triggers (penetration test and disclosure policy before the first real-data pilot; SOC 2 Type I readiness at the first paying customer). `VPS-003`'s Enterprise plan gains customer-managed keys and residency choice.
