### F206 — support and system principals have no audit shape and no policy rows

Raised 21 September 2026, building Stage 3 of the server-authoritative build.

**What is specified.** `VPS-A004` A004-T22 requires support and system principals to be evaluated by the interceptor against their own policy rows, and every decision they receive to be audited. `VPS-A004` and `VPS-A003` name four system principals (`audience-recompute`, `retention-sweep`, `erasure`, `key-rotation`).

**What is not.**
- **The audit shape.** The `AuditEntry` contract in `VPS-F004` records an actor as `actor_user_id` and `actor_membership_id`, both UUIDs of a person. A support grant has neither, and a system job is not a user. There is no field that can name them.
- **The support scope.** `VPS-A008` A008-T02 says an access request states "scope" and `VPS-A008` describes it as which workspace areas, read or write. "Areas" is not defined against the registry, so a support principal's policy row cannot be written.
- **System operations.** No document lists what each system principal may do, so "permitted operations are enumerated in the policy table" has nothing to enumerate.

**What was built without deciding.** The `Principal` union has all three kinds. The policy table holds the system principals' names and one operation, `audit.pseudonymize-actor` for `erasure`, because that operation has code and `VPS-F004` G06 states it. A support or system principal resolves to no node grant, and asking the interceptor to audit one throws `NonMemberAuditUnsupportedError`, so nothing is ever released without its entry.

**Closed by founder-delegated decision, 21 September 2026.** The recommendation was accepted, with three additions.
1. `AuditEntry` gains `actor_kind` (`member`, `support`, `system`). `actor_user_id` and `actor_membership_id` stay required for a member; a support entry carries `actor_grant_id`; a system entry carries `actor_system_name` from the closed enum. `audit_journal` gained the same columns and a check that exactly the matching identifier is present. **`VPS-F004`'s AuditEntry contract was corrected accordingly** (new G08).
2. A support scope is `{ access: "read" | "read-write", node_types }`, evaluated against the same policy table as a member and capped at the scope, never above Owner. A support principal may never write `Workspace`, `WorkspaceMembership`, `User` or `AuditEntry`, never change roles, and never reach a Tier 3 type, whatever its scope lists. `VPS-A008`'s "areas" means node types.
3. Each stage adds the system operations it builds: Stage 5 adds `key-rotation` and `erasure`'s key destruction; Stage 6 adds `audience-recompute`.
