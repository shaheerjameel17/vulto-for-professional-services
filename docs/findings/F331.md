### F331 — the accrual engine's details are unspecified

**Found while:** briefing Stage 27 (`VRS-F018`), 26 September 2026, by checking the spec against the code before writing the brief.

The spec leaves several engine details open that a correct implementation must fix: how a partial first month is prorated (`G05` forbids calendar-day arithmetic, so it cannot be by calendar days), which days carry-over expiry consumes first, what `expiringSoon` means, and what "a clear explanation" of a negative balance is.

**Files:** `docs/Vulto_Specs/VRS-F018_Leave_Policy_Engine.md`

**Ruling.** **Closed 26 September 2026 by ruling.** (a) A partial first month accrues `entitlement ÷ 12 × (working days from start date to month end ÷ working days in that month)`, both counted through the employee's working calendar, and only once that month is complete; the month in progress accrues nothing. (b) Usage is attributed by date and consumes carried-over days first, then the current year's entitlement; carried days unused at their expiry (`policy-year start + carryover_expiry_days`) stop counting. (c) `expiringSoon` is the unused carried-over days that expire within 30 days after the as-of date. (d) A negative `remaining` is returned with a `notes` entry stating the cause (usage exceeds accrual to date, by how many days, under which method); the engine never clamps it silently. (e) Every date is a UTC calendar date; every count is working days.
