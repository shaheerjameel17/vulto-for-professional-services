### F338 — an `Earned` balance has no defined semantics

**Found while:** briefing Stage 28, 27 September 2026.

The spec gives the `Earned` rule in one line (accumulated from approved overtime, expiring `toil_expiry_days` after accrual, capped at `toil_max_accrued_days`) but does not say how the cap counts, whether TOIL rolls at the policy year, or which accrual a deduction consumes.

**Files:** `packages/schema/src/leave-balance.ts`, `docs/Vulto_Specs/VRS-F018_Leave_Policy_Engine.md`

**Ruling.** **Closed 27 September 2026 by ruling.** TOIL does not follow the policy year and has no carry-over: each ledger entry counts from its `effective_date` until its `expires_on` and then stops. The cap applies at accrual: `granted = min(requested, max(0, toil_max_accrued_days − unexpired remaining))`, so expiry frees headroom. Deduction (usage, when `VRS-F019` supplies it) consumes the entries live on the request's start date, earliest expiry first. `entitledYtd` is the days accrued in the as-of UTC calendar year, `carriedOver` the still-live days accrued earlier, `expiringSoon` the live days expiring within 30 days, and a negative remaining carries a note, as for every type. Stage 27's "Earned returns zero with a note" branch is replaced.
