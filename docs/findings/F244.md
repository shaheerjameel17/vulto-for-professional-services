### F244 — `VRS-F006`'s NFRs and System States describe Tier 1 data as syncing to and aging out on devices

Raised 23 September 2026 while checking the code directly before writing the Stage 14 brief, before any product code was written.

**The gap.** `VRS-F006`'s Non-Functional Requirements state "RateCard and RateCardLine sync only to Owner, Finance Admin and HR Admin devices per `VPS-A003`," its System States table gives "Aged out" the treatment "Superseded cards outside `VPS-A003`'s Tier 1 window render dashed with **Fetch**," and its Depends-On line cites `VPS-A003` for "Tier 1 sync and encryption." `VPS-A003` itself says the opposite of all three: Tier 1's device state is "Memory only, fetched on demand; never persisted to device storage," and a later section states plainly that "Tier 1 recovery holders, the threshold scheme and the retention window are withdrawn... `tier1_retention_window_months` is deprecated." There is no sync, no per-role device cache, and no window for anything to age out of — every Tier 1 read is a fresh, authorized, audited fetch, full stop. This is the identical class of error F236 already found and corrected in `VRS-F005`, left uncaught here because `VRS-F006` was drafted to the same pre-`F199` assumptions and never re-swept once the architecture pivoted.

**Closed by founder-delegated decision, 23 September 2026.**
1. The NFR bullet is corrected to: "RateCard and RateCardLine are Tier 1 — fetched on demand by Owner, Finance Admin and HR Admin roles through the permission interceptor, and never cached or persisted on any device."
2. The System States table's "Aged out" row is corrected to "Not applicable. Every read is fetched fresh; nothing is cached on-device to age out of" — the same answer `VRS-F005` already gives its own Tier 2 Contextual Intelligence Panel content.
3. The Depends-On line's "Tier 1 sync and encryption" is corrected to "Tier 1 fetch-on-demand and encryption."

**Status: closed.** Stage 14 is unblocked; nothing in the brief implies building a sync or replication mechanism for RateCard/RateCardLine that `F199`'s architecture does not have.
