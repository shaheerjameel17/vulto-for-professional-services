### F346 — a local Skill row has no place in the fixed palette groups

**Found while:** the builder's pre-build trace of UI-1, 27 September 2026.

`VPS-F002` says a Skill-name match also shows the matcher's results "alongside any direct name matches", but its fixed group order (and `COMMAND_PALETTE_GROUPS`) has no group for the Skill row itself; **Skill matches** is the server's ranked people. Rendering the local Skill row inside **Skill matches** would mix two kinds of result (a skill and the people who have it) under one header.

**Files:** `docs/Vulto_Specs/VPS-F002_Local-First_Search.md`, `packages/ui/src/CommandPalette.tsx`

**Ruling.** **Closed 27 September 2026 by ruling.** A distinct **Skills** group between **People** and **Skill matches**: the direct name matches (badge `Skill`, context line the skill's category). **Skill matches** stays the server's ranked people. Choosing a Skill row opens the existing panel with its context, as any result without a page does; a skill page is a later concern. `VPS-F002`'s order becomes Commands, People, Skills, Skill matches, Projects, Clients, Documents, Policies and `COMMAND_PALETTE_GROUPS` gains `Skills` (builder edits the component; reviewer edited the spec).
