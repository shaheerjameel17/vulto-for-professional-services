### F309 — only two of the eleven MVP notification rules have a built event source

**Found while:** briefing Stage 25 (`VPS-F003`), 26 September 2026, by checking the spec against the code before writing the brief.

Of the spec's eleven MVP rules only two have a built, event-driven source: RevenueGapAlert created or escalated (Stage 20) and TimesheetAnomalyFlag created (Stage 18). Six depend on unbuilt features (`VRS-F019` leave, `VRS-F021` e-signature, `VRS-F023` probation and departure, `VRS-F024` onboarding); the other three (`VRS-F010` week-end not submitted, `VRS-F004` provisional holiday window, `VRS-F008` weekly sweep result) need a scheduler, and there is no jobs service (FDN-57). Separately, Stage 18 deferred `hrCompliance.sendReminder` until this feature exists (`VRS-F010`'s Out of Scope, and Stage 18's report).

**Files:** `docs/Vulto_Specs/VPS-F003_Notification_and_Alert_Center.md`, `docs/Vulto_Specs/VRS-F010_Timesheet_Speed-Run.md`, `docs/stage-reports/STAGE-18_Timesheet_Speed-Run.md`

**Ruling.** **Closed 26 September 2026 by ruling, on the spec's own "the registry is open" clause.** The registry ships with exactly the two event-driven rules; the rest register with the features that build them or with FDN-57. Stage 18's deferred hook is delivered here as a named mutation, `hrCompliance.sendReminder`, whose one boundary is the spec's "the employee themselves": it notifies the employee about their own week, from an Owner or HR Admin (authorization refined by F315). It is a manual producer, not a scheduled rule, so the week-end scheduled rule stays deferred.
