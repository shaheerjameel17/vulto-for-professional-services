# FDN-52 Stage 6 — Requirements-to-Evidence Audit

**Status:** Final Founder-ruling implementation checkpoint, 21 August 2026. F172–F175 and regression-driven F180–F181 have direct implementation evidence. F167 still blocks SLIP-0039 recovery, no recovery implementation was added, and FDN-52 must not be marked complete.

**Founder ruling after F167:** Stage 6 was split into **6A**, intended to close the non-SLIP-0039 work, and **6B**, which qualifies one genuine Worker-compatible SLIP-0039 implementation and prepares its independent-audit and interoperability gates. That ruling still prohibits any unaudited candidate from production dependencies or runtime code. This QA pass found additional non-SLIP gaps; they are not retroactively assigned to F167.

## Classification key

- **Proven directly** — FDN-52 implementation and direct test evidence exist.
- **Dependency/interface** — FDN-52 exposes or consumes the named boundary; the owning issue proves the other side.
- **Out of scope** — the requirement belongs wholly to the named owner.
- **Remaining gap** — direct FDN-52 work is absent or weaker than the governing text.
- **Known limitation** — honest assurance/environment limitation that does not change the implemented contract.

## Approved F164 ruling

F164's cryptographic construction is directly exercised by `tier3-root.ts`, `tier3-partitions.ts`, their unit suites, and the real Chromium lifecycle: exact `(workspace, canonical subject, construction)` root identity; separate RFC 8785 root header; fresh 32-byte PRF input; domain-separated PRF/recovery HKDF; subject-only confirmed enrollment/recovery; one credential; fresh root/code/envelopes and document epochs; and lazy historical ciphertext. Adversarial QA added authoritative-PRF-envelope and cross-document-history validation. F173 prevents the durable generation lineage from being called rollback-safe across Workers or cold restart. F154 remains a **Known limitation**: a Chromium CTAP2.1 virtual authenticator proves standards-level PRF wiring, not native platform-authenticator interoperability.

## Regenerated result

Adversarial QA closed four concrete restore/binding defects (F168–F171), two specification drifts (F176/F179), one malformed durable PRF-input defect (F177), and one partial-failure key-lifetime defect (F178). Founder rulings now resolve F172–F175 at the specification/ownership layer, without treating implementation as complete. F167 remains the only SLIP-0039 blocker; F172–F175 require their direct implementation and destructive/production-surface evidence before final assurance.

Relay/TLS/server opacity remains FDN-51; device/membership erase delivery remains FDN-63/FDN-87; protected bootstrap remains FDN-88; concrete identity/relationship/subject-exclusion resolution remains FDN-89; query enforcement remains FDN-53; erasure approval/audit semantics remains VPS-F007. F173 and F175 cross an FDN-84 boundary, and F174 requires an owning-specification correction for Tier 2, but those dependencies are now named gaps rather than evidence of completion.
